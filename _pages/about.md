I'm **Sanjana Nambiar**, an AI security researcher interested in making LLMs and generative AI systems more reliable, robust, and safe. I'm especially excited about adversarial behavior, trustworthy AI, and uncovering where (and why!) language models fail. Beyond security, I'm curious about what makes LLMs tick; from mechanistic interpretability and reasoning to the reliability of agentic AI systems.

I'm currently a **Junior Research Scientist** at the [Center for Interacting Urban Networks (CITIES) Research Institute](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/research/faculty-labs-and-projects/center-for-interacting-urban-networks.html) at **NYU Abu Dhabi**, working with [Prof. Azza Abouzied](https://azzaabouzied.com/) and [Prof. Christina Pöpper](https://poepper.net/).
