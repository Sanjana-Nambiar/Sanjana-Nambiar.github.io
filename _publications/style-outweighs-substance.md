This paper introduces SOS-Bench (Substance Outweighs Style Benchmark), a standardized meta-benchmark for evaluating LLM alignment. It demonstrates that LLM-judge preferences often prioritize style over factual accuracy and safety, and highlights the importance of supervised fine-tuning, data scaling, and prompt diversity in improving alignment.
